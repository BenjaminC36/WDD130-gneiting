Welcome to Dragons & Phoenixes

Welcome to the world of mythology. Throughout history there have been tales of incredible creatures with inhuman abilities. Among the most famous are Dragons with their ability to torment towns and breathe fire and Phoenixes with the legendary ability to be reborn. Here you can explore legends and their unique abilities to learn just what made them so famous.

Fun Facts

Dragons

In Chinese mythology, dragons are often considered symbols of good luck, wisdom, and power rather than dangerous fire-breathing monsters. They are also associated with controlling the rain and water.

Phoenix

The inspiration for the phoenix came from the Egyptian Bennu bird which was associated with the sun, God Ra, creation, and renewal.
